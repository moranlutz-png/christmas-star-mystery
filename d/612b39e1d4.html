Reveal the thief (Day 24)

The thief

Suspect 17, MIA MITCHELL, 29, observatory assistant, stole the Christmas Star.

Why she stole it

While cataloguing the Observatory's old star charts, Mia asked librarian Sophie Williams to help translate an old scroll lent out on 15 November. Sophie translated the start. Mia finished it herself and decoded the Awakening Scroll: whoever touches the Christmas Star when it is fully awakened will absorb all its power and become the most powerful person in the world. The Star fully awakens at midnight on Christmas Eve, when the aurora crowns the dome. Mia, who felt overlooked in the village, wanted to be the most powerful person in the world – and, above all, to be noticed.

How she gained access

Mia really did leave the tower at 00:50, as she said. But her observatory staff key let her back in by the dome service stair at 01:15. She tripped the dome breaker, which caused the Blackout and blinded the cameras, then opened the Star Vault at 01:20 with the observatory calibration key kept by the assistant. She wrapped the Star in the gold Secret Santa paper from Henry Mitchell's Wrapping Room (her uncle, entirely innocent: Day 10) and left at 01:50 as the lights returned.

What the fingerprint evidence proves

The print on the Vault glass is a double loop from a LEFT thumb, and the scratches on the Vault lock were made by a left-handed pick. With the size 5 boot mark in the Vault dust and a smear at 160–168 cm, only Lucy Harrison (#15) and Mia fit. Mia is left-handed, with a double-loop print, size 5 boots and a height of 164 cm.

Why Lucy Harrison (#15) was innocent

Lucy has the same print type, the same boot size and almost the same height, and she looked guilty: the Blackout began while she was the lighting technician, and she has roof-light access. But she is right-handed, and the generator log shows her key-fob resetting the breaker at Lighting Control every five minutes from 01:25 to 01:50, so she could not have been in the tower. The auburn hair on the Vault carpet (the rarest hair colour in the village) was left on 29 November while she changed the Vault bulbs.

The red herrings

Henry's gold paper (given to every department), the glove button (Charlie's village gloves), Alexander's visitor map, the sleigh wax (Edward waxed the lift), the star stamp, the star gift box, the child's letter, the toy train wheel, the sugar dust, Jessica's roof-door log, and Bravo's flight over the tower all had innocent explanations.

A Christmas ending

At midnight on Christmas Eve, Santa, the elves and our detective found Mia in the dome with the Star in her hands – untouched, because she had been waiting for the aurora. Mia confessed everything. As the aurora crowned the dome the Star woke at last, and its real power was revealed: it makes everyone near it feel noticed and loved. Santa forgave Mia and asked her to lift the Star to the top of the village tree. The Christmas Star shines for everyone. Merry Christmas, detective!
